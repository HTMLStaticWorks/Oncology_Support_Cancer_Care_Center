PATIENT QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common inquiries regarding appointments, triage access, treatments, and insurance coverage.

How quickly can a new patient schedule an appointment?

We prioritize newly diagnosed patients and second opinion requests within 24 to 48 hours. Our nurse navigators assist with expediting medical records and pathology reports.

Do I need a physician referral to be seen?

While physician referrals are common, we gladly accept self-referrals for consultations, chemotherapy infusion care, radiation coordination, and survivorship evaluations.

What should I bring to my initial consultation?

Please bring government-issued photo ID, active insurance cards, prior diagnostic imaging discs/reports, a list of current medications and supplements, and any questions for the care team.

How does the 24/7 urgent oncology triage work?

Our 24/7 triage hotline is staffed by Oncology Certified Nurses (OCN) who can assess acute chemotherapy side effects, fever, pain, or infusion reactions and arrange immediate intervention.

What insurance and financial assistance options exist?

We participate with Medicare, Medicaid, and major commercial health plans. Dedicated financial navigators also help patients explore co-pay assistance, grants, and pharmaceutical support.

Can a family member or caregiver accompany me?

Yes, family members and caregivers are warmly welcomed and encouraged to join both consultation visits and chemotherapy infusion sessions in our spacious suites.
