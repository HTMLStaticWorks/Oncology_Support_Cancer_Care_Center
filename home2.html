COMMON PATIENT QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear guidance on consultations, insurance coverage, treatment schedules, and supportive services.

We prioritize all new oncology referrals and second opinions with our 48-Hour Priority Intake Guarantee. You will be matched with a dedicated oncology nurse navigator who collects your records, schedules initial imaging or lab tests, and confirms your multi-specialist appointment within two business days.

Most major private insurance plans, Medicare, and Medicare Advantage cover Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS) and biomarker testing when medically indicated. For clinical trials, routine patient care costs are covered by insurance under federal mandates, while investigational drugs are typically provided at no cost by trial sponsors.

Our tumor board brings together medical oncologists, radiation oncologists, surgical oncologists, molecular pathologists, and radiologists. Together, they review your diagnostic imaging, pathology slides, and genomic markers collaboratively to reach a unanimous consensus on the most effective treatment protocol.

Yes. Our private and semi-private infusion suites are designed to comfortably accommodate you and a caregiver. Each suite includes companion seating, Wi-Fi, heated blankets, and complimentary refreshments to ensure both patient and loved one feel supported.

Scalp cooling systems (such as Paxman and DigniCap) gently reduce the temperature of the scalp before, during, and after chemotherapy infusion. This constricts blood vessels in hair follicles, significantly reducing the amount of chemotherapy drug reaching them and substantially lowering hair loss rates.
